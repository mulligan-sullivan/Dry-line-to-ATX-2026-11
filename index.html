comfort edge (55 F dew point)
The edge is where the dew point crosses 55 F, interpolated between stations (splitting the difference between the two stations it falls between). The path follows the forecast track of the front itself: from the head of the dry sector at Colorado Springs, down the high plains to Amarillo and Childress, then through Wichita Falls and Fort Worth to Austin. Comfort is defined as dew point at or below 55 F and temperature at or below 79 F. There is no lower temperature limit: too cold is fine. The status line reports where comfort is met on the path. Data: Open-Meteo, current conditions plus hourly forecast for Austin. Auto-refreshes every 10 minutes.
